Quidditch Manager is a free, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by
J.K. Rowling, Warner Bros. Entertainment, Bloomsbury or Wizarding World Digital. Harry Potter, Quidditch, and the
names of the teams, players, schools and broomsticks taken from the books are their property and are used here only

The game tries to stay faithful to the books and to *Quidditch Through the Ages*. Everything that does not come from
them is marked as invented: `source: "filler"` in `data/`, `[FILLER]` in `docs/` and in code comments. Gameplay rules
chosen for the game (not in the books) are marked `[game]`.

## Flags and maps
- `assets/flags/*.png`: flags from [flagcdn.com](https://flagcdn.com) (public domain).
- `assets/art/map_world.png`, `assets/art/map_europe.png`: drawn by `tools/make_map.py` from
  [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) coastlines (public domain, `tools/map_data/*.geojson`).

## Crests, characters and broomsticks
- `assets/crests/*.svg`: original vector crests generated by `tools/make_crests.py` (shield, club colours and the
  emblem described in *Quidditch Through the Ages*, chapter 7). `assets/crests/patched/` and `assets/crests/fx/` are
  the embroidered versions and textures made from them by `tools/patch_crests.gd`.
- `assets/crests/ai/*.png`, `assets/portraits/*.png`, `assets/brooms/*.png`: original artwork generated with ChatGPT
  for this project from the prompts in `docs/art/`, then cut out by `tools/import_crest_sheets.gd`.
- `assets/art/logo.png`, `assets/art/prophet.png`, `assets/art/paper_*.png`, `assets/icon.png`: generated by
  `tools/make_logo.gd`, `tools/make_prophet.gd`, `tools/make_paper.gd` and `tools/make_icon.gd`.

## Sound
- `assets/sounds/*.wav`: synthesised by `tools/make_sounds.gd` and `tools/make_music.gd` (whistle, goal, save,
  Bludger, Snitch, crowd, click and the music themes). No third-party material.

## Website (`server/static/`)
- Copies of the fonts above with their OFL texts, `icon.png`/`logo.png` (original), and `map_world.jpg`/`map_europe.jpg`, JPEG versions of the Natural Earth maps listed under "Flags and maps".

## Data
- `server/data/geo/`: region and city names and coordinates from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org)
  (`admin1CodesASCII.txt`, `cities5000.zip`), licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); trimmed by `server/scripts/build-geo.ts`.

## Third-party artwork included in this repository
These files are not original to this project and are **not** covered by the MIT license. They remain the property of
their owners and are included only as a non-commercial tribute. If you are a rights holder and want any of them
removed, please open an issue. Without them the game falls back to its own crests and hides the other images.

- `assets/art/title_bg.jpg`: a still from the Harry Potter films. © Warner Bros. Entertainment.
- `assets/art/world_cup.png`: the Quidditch World Cup trophy (Harry Potter Wiki / Warner Bros.).
- `assets/crests/ai/club_<13 British and Irish clubs>.png` and `assets/portraits/club_<same 13 clubs>.png`: crests and
  uniform portraits generated with AI, based on Emily Gravett's illustrations for the illustrated edition of
  *Quidditch Through the Ages* (Bloomsbury, 2020).
- `assets/crests/ai/hs_hogwarts_*.png`: house crests generated with AI, based on the Hogwarts house crests
  (Pottermore / Warner Bros.).

The official crests themselves (Emily Gravett's patches and the Pottermore house crests) are not in this repository.
