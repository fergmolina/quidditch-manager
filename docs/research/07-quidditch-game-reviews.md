## 1. Game by game

### 1.1 Harry Potter: Quidditch World Cup (2003, EA / Magic Pockets; PS2, Xbox, GC, PC, GBA)

**What it is.** It has three modes: Hogwarts (House Cup, with six tutorial challenges), World Cup (nine national teams: England, USA, Japan, Germany, France, Australia, Nordic Team, Spain, and Bulgaria), and Exhibition. The Snitch works in two phases: the Chasers fill a meter with passes, combos, and goals, and only once the meter is full does the Seeker chase begin. **The Snitch is worth 150 and in practice wins the match.** The Hogwarts Cup **is decided by accumulated points, not just by wins**. The rosters match Harry's third year. Hogwarts matches are commentated by Lee Jordan and Seamus, and international ones by Ludo Bagman. There are collectible cards that unlock special moves. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter:_Quidditch_World_Cup))

**Reception.** Metascore of 68 on PS2 and GC, 69 on Xbox and PC, and 53 on GBA. Metacritic users: 6.8 from 43 ratings. ([Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/))

**Professional critics: praise**
- GMR: "so much fun (for fans and nonfans alike) that it makes us wish Quidditch were actually real" (80). ([Metacritic, critic reviews](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/critic-reviews/))
- Game Informer (78) described it as "technically solid, entertaining, original". EGM (72) highlighted its "instinctive, responsive gameplay". ([ibid.](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/critic-reviews/))
- GameSpot (6.5) praised each stadium having its own identity: the Japanese pitch over a koi pond, the Nordic one among glaciers. Also the team special moves with cinematics. **[snippet]** ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup-review/1900-6083400/))

**Professional critics: complaints**
- IGN (72, PS2) complained that "the forgiving AI" lets you progress too easily, and about "the development team's decision to eliminate vertical control". ([Metacritic, critic reviews](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/critic-reviews/)) A search snippet attributed a 60 to IGN with the phrase "shallow game design". I could not verify it and it may be a different platform.
- Next Level Gaming (81): "way to easy in the single player mode". Deeko (70): "isn't as deep as other EA sporting games", which shortens its lifespan for adult players. Worth Playing (84): "not as advanced as I had hoped". ([ibid.](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/critic-reviews/))
- GameSpot criticized the "sluggish" controls, the fact that special moves cannot be blocked (which takes away strategy), and **"extremely repetitive"** commentary. **[snippet]** ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup-review/1900-6083400/))
- Off The Shelf Media, in a 2024 retrospective, gave it 5/10: "I've played over 100 Quidditch matches, and I've never been behind". It also pointed out that "There is no difference between playing as Gryffindor or Ravenclaw", that there is "nothing to unlock besides the wizard cards", and that the players are "a copy-and-paste recreation of each other". ([offtheshelfmedia](https://offtheshelfmedia.com/2024/09/17/ea-caught-the-snitch-but-lost-the-match-harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup-review/))

**Users.** Opinion is split. Some call it "the ultimate gaming experience for any fan" and others say "you learn a way to win every time. No replay value at all". ([Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/)) The GameFAQs reviews could not be opened (403). One of the titles that showed up in search sums up the typical complaint: "Good but feel bored after a few matches". **[snippet]** ([GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/gamecube/914743-harry-potter-quidditch-world-cup/reviews/66556))

**Takeaway.** It is the game that came closest to canon (seven players, 150-point Snitch, Cup decided by accumulated points, commentators from the books). It failed on depth, difficulty, differences between teams, and progression, not on the rules.

### 1.2 Harry Potter: Quidditch Champions (2024, Unbroken Studios / WB Games)

**What it is.** There are four interchangeable positions. **It changes the canon rules:** each team has six players with a single Beater, **the Snitch is worth 30 and does not end the match**, and you win by reaching 100 or when time runs out, with a golden goal if tied. The "career" mode is three tournaments: House Cup, Triwizard Cup, and a World Cup with 16 countries. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter:_Quidditch_Champions))

**Reception.** Metascore of 66 on PS5, 64 on Xbox Series X, and 63 on PC. 84% of reviews are mixed. Metacritic users: 5.4. On OpenCritic it averages 66 and only 28% of critics recommend it. On Steam it is rated "Mostly Positive" (72% of 1,709 reviews). ([Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-champions/), [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/17116/harry-potter-quidditch-champions), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2878600/Harry_Potter_Quidditch_Champions/)) It lost 48% of its PSN players in its second week ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter:_Quidditch_Champions)). ScreenRant reports it peaked at 3,977 players and dropped to 200-300 daily ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/hogwarts-legacy-dlc-doesnt-need-quidditch/)). There were patches at least until February and March 2025 **[snippet]**. I found no official end-of-support announcement.

**Professional critics: praise**
- IGN (6/10): "When you're flying around the pitch, Harry Potter: Quidditch Champions can be a blast" ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/17116/harry-potter-quidditch-champions)).
- ScreenRant (8/10) applauded the Snitch change because it keeps "other positions [...] relevant". It also highlighted that there is "plenty to unlock and absolutely no microtransactions" and that Durmstrang and Beauxbatons appear. ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-ps5-review/))
- TheSixthAxis (6/10): "Being a Beater is terrific fun"; "boosting along on your broomstick feels just the right amount of punchy". ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2024/09/12/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/))
- Game Rant (7/10) said the positions strike "a nice balance between accessible and engaging" ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/)). Gameindustry.com wrote that in matches it "very much hits the high points in both depth of gameplay and fun" ([gameindustry](https://www.gameindustry.com/reviews/game-review/grabbing-quaffles-and-snitches-in-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/)).
- Hardcore Gamer (3/5) said of the Seeker: "nail-biter moments" ([Hardcore Gamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/)).

**Professional critics: complaints**
- IGN: "there just isn't much off of it to keep you playing long-term". Shacknews (7): "The gameplay foundation is solid, but there just isn't much done on top of it". ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/17116/harry-potter-quidditch-champions))
- **The Seeker is boring.** TheSixthAxis: "fly back and forth along the pitch [...] waiting for a meter to fill". VideoGamer (5/10): "catching the Snitch perhaps the most tedious part of the game. You have to follow it in a set path back and forth for about a minute". ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2024/09/12/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/), [VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/reviews/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/))
- **Repetition.** VideoGamer: "you're effectively just playing the same match over and over again". Hardcore Gamer: "The game gets tedious quickly". WayTooManyGames (7/10): "I never felt like playing more than two or three matches at a time". ([VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/reviews/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/), [Hardcore Gamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/), [WayTooManyGames](https://waytoomany.games/2024/09/09/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/))
- **Capped progression.** Game Rant: "once those 10 skill points have been earned, there's no more character progression", and that is reached in "only a few hours". ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/))
- **Nothing outside the match.** Gameindustry: "when you aren't playing a match, then the game is generally quite dull". VideoGamer called it "a minigame that's been bafflingly given a full release". ([gameindustry](https://www.gameindustry.com/reviews/game-review/grabbing-quaffles-and-snitches-in-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/), [VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/reviews/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-review/))
- **Arenas without identity.** Hardcore Gamer: the World Cup is played on a single map, "dull to look at". ([Hardcore Gamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/))
- **Commentary is missing.** ScreenRant missed "the beloved sassy commentary of Lee Jordan". ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-ps5-review/))
- **Canon.** WayTooManyGames noted that with the Snitch at 30 the Seeker is "underwhelming". Hardcore Gamer criticized Sebastian Sallow appearing outside his era: "It makes no sense he's alive during this time frame". ([WayTooManyGames](https://waytoomany.games/2024/09/09/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/), [Hardcore Gamer](https://hardcoregamer.com/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/))
- Other complaints: the Keeper is "rubbish" and the camera "can't keep up" (TheSixthAxis). Hard AI turns into "a stalemate" (Game Rant).

**Users**
- On Metacritic the recurring complaints are dated graphics ("looks exactly the same as Quidditch World Cup from 2003"), imprecise controls, and thin single player. A frequent comment is also that it should have been Hogwarts Legacy DLC. ([Metacritic users](https://www.metacritic.com/game/harry-potter-quidditch-champions/user-reviews/))
- Steam, negative reviews: "Doesn't reflect the canon Quidditch game at all" (80 h played), "Always online suck" (225 h), plus complaints about the camera and tackling. The positive ones talk about "quick, fun matches" and that it's worth it "on a sale". The most repeated complaint in the positive reviews is the lack of online players. ([Steam API, negative](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/2878600?json=1&filter=all&language=english&num_per_page=40&review_type=negative), [positive](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/2878600?json=1&filter=all&language=english&num_per_page=40&review_type=positive))
- **Thread "This game needs a real career mode".** They ask for progression "like 2K's MyPlayer" across **seven seasons at Hogwarts**, then cups and professional leagues. One replied "beat the game in four hours". ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2878600/discussions/0/4757578278657881734/))
- **Suggestions thread.** They ask for "A REAL Career mode where you create a team and compete in national league", moving from school to league teams and the World Cup, more national teams with their own rosters and stadiums, "detailed player statistics", and offline play. ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2878600/discussions/0/4849903351690647122/))
- **Snitch debate.** In favor of the change: "150 points AND it ends the game? That means to lose [...] the other team needs to score 15 times." Against: "if they didn't want the game to end quickly then make it harder and add more depth to the snitch gameplay", and "By nerfing that role to oblivion [...] the game is missing out on [...] the same HP fans who made Hogwarts Legacy a success." ([Steam, Snitch rework thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2878600/discussions/0/4757578099468590733/?ctp=2))
- In the Push Square comments most supported the change, but some "traditionalists" asked for the option to choose the original rules, especially in single player. The developers said the original rules were "broken" for competitive play. ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2024/08/harry-potter-quidditch-champions-snitch-has-been-dramatically-nerfed-on-ps5-ps4))

**Takeaway.** Flying and the Beater are entertaining, but the match wears thin quickly, there is no career, there is no team identity, and the canon was broken to balance the action. Users are asking, in so many words, for a management game with a long career.

### 1.3 Hogwarts Legacy (2023): the missing Quidditch

- WB confirmed before launch that there would be no Quidditch. The in-game justification is that the season was canceled because of an injury in the previous final. Kotaku points out that there are upgradable brooms, uniforms, and a pitch with accessible stands, all of which made people expect the sport. ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/hogwarts-legacy-no-quidditch-dlc-canceled-reddit-1850173984))
- Reddit reactions, collected by the press because Reddit could not be opened directly: "My disappointment is immeasurable and my day is ruined" and "literally as important to harrys character as his wand is". There were threads with over a thousand upvotes suspecting it had been cut to sell separately. **[GamesRadar snippet; article truncated when opened]** ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/hogwarts-legacy-fans-call-out-the-new-quidditch-game-for-being-merely-cut-content/))
- When Quidditch Champions was announced: "What a waste of resource, this could have been part of Hogwarts Legacy" and "Welp…now we know why it wasn't in Legacy". ([PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2023/04/18/quidditch-champions-hogwarts-legacy-fans-reaction/))
- **The opposite view exists too.** ScreenRant argues that "Quidditch isn't actually that fun of a game to play". Either you chase an unpredictable Snitch or you wait for the AI to finish the match. It adds that in canon Quidditch "is actually canonically only played a few times throughout the school year". ([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/hogwarts-legacy-dlc-doesnt-need-quidditch/))

**Takeaway.** Demand for Quidditch is huge and emotional. ScreenRant's argument that playing Quidditch first-person is boring does not apply to a manager, where the player does not chase the Snitch. They make decisions.

### 1.4 Quidditch minigames in the movie tie-in games

- **Philosopher's Stone (PC, 2001).** The player is only the Seeker. They fly through rings left by the Snitch to fill a meter and then try to catch it. If they run out of stamina from collisions, the team loses. One critic said the matches become "as repetitive and dull as the rest of the game after a couple". Another considered the difficulty "negligible" because the other players have no influence on the result. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Philosopher%27s_Stone_(PC_video_game)))
- **Chamber of Secrets (PC, 2002).** The broom flies itself and the player only bumps the opposing Seeker. Ragglefraggle: "its boring, it has no challenge at all". ([Ragglefraggle Reviews](https://ragglefragglereviews.com/2017/03/13/harry-potter-and-the-chamber-of-secrets-pc-2002-review/)) I found no reviews dedicated to the Quidditch in the console version of CoS. The only thing is a favorable comparison WayTooManyGames makes with the GameCube controls ([WayTooManyGames](https://waytoomany.games/2024/09/09/review-harry-potter-quidditch-champions/)).
- **Prisoner of Azkaban (2004).** Quidditch is not playable: it appears only in cutscenes, and the Buckbeak flights fill that role. **[TV Tropes snippet; page 403]**. The game's Wikipedia article does not mention Quidditch ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Prisoner_of_Azkaban_(video_game))).
- **Half-Blood Prince (2009).** Quidditch returns with tryouts and matches, with Harry as captain. Wikipedia records no specific criticism of that part. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Half-Blood_Prince_(video_game)))

**Takeaway.** Reducing Quidditch to the Seeker produces matches where the rest of the team doesn't matter, and that is exactly what critics pointed out.

### 1.5 Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery (mobile, Quidditch since 2019)

- Quidditch was added on September 5, 2019, starting from Year 2, Chapter 6. "Friendlies" matches are tasks where you drag the Quaffle to circles within 12 seconds, graded Perfect, Great, or Good. The reviewer welcomed the addition, because the game "did feel like it was missing something". ([Mae Polzine](https://maepolzine.com/blog/hogwarts-mystery-quidditch-has-arrived/))
- There are complaints about waits of up to 15 hours between tasks (energy and timers). **[snippet; AppGamer returned 403]**

**Takeaway.** It shows that just "having Quidditch" is enough for players to value it, but also that mobile timers make it tedious. It adds nothing to the depth of the sport.

### 1.6 Fan games and Quidditch managers

This is the most relevant part for us. **The fan projects that survive are management simulators with statistics, not action games.**

- **Quidditch Manager / Quidditch Simulator (ChaoticPlay, itch.io, Unity).** It has 22 teams (4 Houses, 8 from Britain and Ireland, and 10 international) and Exhibition, Season, Playoffs, and Management modes (create a team, sign free agents, transfers, training, stadium upgrades). Weather affects each team, home advantage gives 3-5%, there are more than 20 stadiums, and a momentum graph. One of its patches fixed "Seekers were able to catch snitch while being knocked out". No public ratings are visible. ([itch.io](https://chaoticplay.itch.io/quidditch-sim), [devlog v2.1](https://chaoticplay.itch.io/quidditch-sim/devlog/818704/quidditch-simulator-v21-content-update))
- **Harry Potter Quidditch Simulator (Faraz, 2025, freeware).** Simulated matches with statistics (Passing, Shooting, Seeking, Saving) and live text commentary **by Lee Jordan**. It is inspired by ICC Cricket Captain and Extreme Warfare Revenge. The Snitch is deliberately random: "Even the best Seekers might miss the snitch occasionally!" Management is planned for later and it has no visible comments. ([farazthewebguy](https://farazthewebguy.com/harry-potter-quidditch-simulator/), [itch.io](https://itch.io/t/5023899/text-based-quidditch-simulator))
- **Quidditch Premier League (Pete Taylour).** An engine that generates events weighted by statistics, with GPT-3.5-generated commentary "reminiscent of" Championship Manager 00/01. **It keeps the 150 rule.** Lessons it leaves: separate the engine from the commentary, and build tension before revealing a Snitch catch. ([petetaylour.world](https://petetaylour.world/qpl/))
- **Quidditch Manager (web game, 2012).** A free online manager of a professional team. According to the search summary, the creator made it because they wanted "a comprehensive manager game with a Quidditch theme and didn't find any". **[snippet; ModDB 403 and blog with no DNS]** ([ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/games/qmanager))

### 1.7 Quadball (the real sport)

In the real version the "snitch" is a runner with a flag. Catching it is worth **30 points** under IQA rules and **35** in the US (MLQ/USQ), and the match ends only if the catching team ends up ahead. Matches last 30 to 40 minutes. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadball)) **The real sport, like Quidditch Champions, also had to shrink the Snitch so the rest of the game would matter.** That is design evidence, but it clashes head-on with the books.

## 3. What people ask for and no game delivered

1. **A truly long career.** "A REAL Career mode where you create a team and compete in national league" and "7 seasons at Hogwarts, followed by the other cups" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2878600/discussions/0/4849903351690647122/), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2878600/discussions/0/4757578278657881734/)).
2. **Professional leagues and a path from school to league to national team.** Requested on Steam, and attempted by fan projects (ChaoticPlay with British teams, QPL, the 2012 web manager). No official game had professional leagues.
3. **A World Cup with identity.** QWC had nine national teams with themed stadiums, which was its most praised feature. QC had sixteen countries on a single "dull" map. People ask for their own rosters and stadiums.
4. **Detailed player statistics** ("detailed player statistics") and real differences between teams and players.
5. **The original rules, at least as an option.** Requested by the Push Square "traditionalists" and the Steam thread.
6. **Lee Jordan's commentary and characters from the books with a real role.** ScreenRant missed it in QC. Faraz uses it. QWC had Lee Jordan, Seamus, and Bagman.
7. **A Quidditch management game.** Nobody asks for it in those words in official reviews, but the fan projects that persist are exactly that, and the creator of the 2012 web manager made it because they couldn't find one **[snippet]**.
8. **Offline play with no live service** ("Always online suck"; offline mode requested in the suggestions thread).

## 4. Recommendations for Quidditch Manager (prioritized)

Legend: **[CANON OK]** fits the faithfulness rule · **[CONFLICTS]** conflicts with the rule of faithfulness to the books · **[CANON-FILLER]** is compatible but requires minimal, marked filler.

### Priority 1: must-haves

1. **Keep the Snitch at 150 and have it end the match, and make the Chasers matter beyond the single match.** **[CANON OK]**
   - *Evidence:* QC dropped it to 30 and the Seeker became "tedious" or "underwhelming", with canon complaints on Steam ("Doesn't reflect the canon", "nerfing that role to oblivion"). QWC kept it and nobody complained about the rule, only about depth.
   - *How:* what carries weight is accumulated points, goal difference, and cup permutations. In canon the House Cup is decided by total points (QWC did it this way), and in *Prisoner of Azkaban* Gryffindor needs to win by a specific margin. That way winning by 160 or by 400 is worth different amounts, and live tactics revolve around "do I catch now or wait to widen the margin?".
   - *Avoid:* shrinking the Snitch, adding a "seeker floor", or a quadball-style time limit **[CONFLICTS]**.

2. **Give the Seeker decisions instead of mechanics.** **[CANON OK]**
   - *Evidence:* in every game the first-person Seeker is boring. Fans ask for "more depth to the snitch gameplay".
   - *How:* orders such as mark the opposing Seeker, search independently, feint (the Wronski Feint is canon), catch now, or wait. The deterministic engine should take into account vision, speed, broom, weather, and fitness, with real uncertainty (like Faraz's "Even the best Seekers might miss").

3. **Long career and full management: seven years at Hogwarts as a youth academy, leagues, European cups, and the World Cup.** **[CANON OK]**
   - *Evidence:* it's the most requested thing ("REAL Career mode", "7 seasons", "national league"). The central complaint against QC is the lack of content outside the match ("dull" or "four hours").

4. **Strong team and player identity.** **[CANON OK / CANON-FILLER]**
   - *Evidence:* "no difference [...] Gryffindor or Ravenclaw" and "copy-and-paste" in QWC; QC with a World Cup on a single map; the themed stadiums were QWC's most praised feature.
   - *How:* individual attributes and playing styles per club and national team; take everything that exists from canon (teams from *Quidditch Through the Ages*, players from the 1994 World Cup) and mark the filler. Stadiums and weather with a real effect (ChaoticPlay already does this with home advantage and weather).

5. **Varied commentary with canon commentators.** **[CANON OK]**
   - *Evidence:* QWC's "extremely repetitive" commentary **[snippet]**, ScreenRant missing Lee Jordan, and Faraz and QPL showing that text sustains interest.
   - *How:* many templates per event, no back-to-back repeats, rising tension before the Snitch (QPL's lesson), context ("needs to win by 200"), and Lee Jordan at Hogwarts. For the professional game, use canon commentators where they exist (Bagman at the World Cup).

### Priority 2: highly recommended

6. **Opponent AI and difficulty that don't give up.** *Evidence:* "forgiving AI" and "never been behind" (QWC), "stalemate" (QC). The AI of the other managers should adapt its tactics and sign players sensibly. **[CANON OK]**
7. **Match length should come out of the simulation.** In canon matches can last days. With 5-second ticks and adjustable speed, long matches aren't tedious and can become stories (fatigue, weather). *Evidence:* repetition and boredom in every action game. **[CANON OK]**
8. **Brooms as a market and a stat, not a difficulty selector.** QWC used them to choose difficulty, and in QC cosmetics have no function. Canon models (Nimbus, Firebolt, etc.) with a cost and an effect in the engine. **[CANON OK / CANON-FILLER]** for the numeric values.
9. **Detailed statistics and history for players and matches.** Explicit request on Steam ("detailed player statistics"). **[CANON OK]**
10. **Characters from the books with a role consistent with their era.** *Evidence:* the criticism of Sebastian Sallow out of his era. Validate dates and ages. **[CANON OK]**
11. **Fully offline, with no live service or timers.** *Evidence:* "Always online suck", low population in QC, 15-hour waits in Hogwarts Mystery **[snippet]**. **[CANON OK]**

### Priority 3: polish

12. **Special moves with a tactical counter.** In QWC they couldn't be blocked, which took away strategy **[snippet]**. If there are named plays (Hawkshead Attacking Formation, Wronski Feint), the opponent's defense should be able to counter them.
13. **A tutorial that teaches the rules, not just the controls.** This is one of ScreenRant's criticisms of QC.
14. **A readable 2D viewer.** *Evidence:* "The camera can't keep up" (TheSixthAxis) and PC Gamer overwhelmed by the amount of stuff on screen **[snippet]**. With a top-down view, use colors per position and highlight the Snitch and the Bludgers.

### What to avoid

- Changing the rules for balance: seven players, two Beaters, a 150-point Snitch that ends the match. QC's approach is the counterexample **[CONFLICTS]** if copied.
- A career of only a few hours, or capped progression.
- Interchangeable teams or national teams, and a single generic stadium.
- Mobile-style timers and energy.
- Commentary with few lines.

### Tensions with the faithfulness rule, made explicit

- **Some players' request to shrink the Snitch** (Push Square, the Steam thread, ScreenRant). **CONFLICTS**. Discarded and compensated with accumulated points and the cup margin.
- **The "seeker floor" and quadball's time limit.** **CONFLICTS**. Discarded.
- **The Seeker deciding too much** is a design risk, not a canon one. Mitigated by giving weight in the standings to the advantage the Chasers build up.
- **Inventing leagues, players, and statistics** that canon doesn't detail. **CANON-FILLER**, minimal and marked, per the project's rule.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. No official Quidditch game got past "mixed". QWC has 68-69 on Metacritic and QC has 63-66 with only 28% recommending it on OpenCritic.
2. What's always fun is flying, contact, stadiums with identity, and being inside the Harry Potter world.
3. What always ruins it is lack of depth, repetition, soft AI, and the absence of progression.
4. The first-person Seeker was boring in every game, from Philosopher's Stone to Champions.
5. QC dropped the Snitch to 30 and ended up with both problems: an irrelevant Seeker and an upset canon community.
6. QWC kept the 150 and the Cup decided by accumulated points, which is the canon model that works for us.
7. Users ask for a long career, seven years at Hogwarts, national leagues, a World Cup with identity, and statistics, and nobody gave them that.
8. The fan projects that persist are management simulators with text commentary, and they validate Quidditch Manager's thesis.
9. Priorities: canon Snitch with weight on accumulated points, Seeker decisions, a full career, team identity, and varied commentary.
10. Avoid rule changes, short careers, interchangeable teams, timers, and mandatory online. Shrinking the Snitch conflicts with the books and is discarded.
